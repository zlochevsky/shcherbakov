<p>Mikhail Shcherbakov, a famous Russian songwriter, will be visiting the USA and Canada with a series of concerts.</p>

<p><a href="../index.html">Go to The Unofficial Scherbakov Page, if you can read Russian.</a></p>

<hr>

<p>Below is the schedule of concerts. Additional concerts may be organized in New York and California. More detailed information will be posted soon.</p>

<p><b>Oct.3</b> - Philadelphia. Contact <a href="mailto:Yuri.Knizhnik@phila.gov">Yuri Knizhnik (215)592-1682</a><br>
<b>Oct.4</b> - Washington. Contact <a href="mailto:iaz@cs.umd.edu">Ilya Zavorin</a>, phone (301)765-0319<br>
<b>Oct.7</b> - Brooklyn, NY. Contact Sergey Moiseev, (718)998-6879</p>

<p><b>Oct.11 - Boston</b>, time 20:00. Contact <a href="mailto:kate@ccs.neu.edu">Katya Klimukhina</a>, (617)738-7290<br>
<b>Oct.13 or 14</b> - Montreal. Contact <a href="mailto:boris@muff.cs.mcgill.ca">Boris Shukhman</a>, (514)767-5089<br>
<b>Oct.15 - Toronto, time 19:00.</b> Contact <a href="mailto:vladimir@atmosp.physics.utoronto.ca">Vladimir Smirnov (416)532-5867</a>, Slava Skorik (416)503-8997<br>
